G1 · What information does your bot use?

G3 · How do you keep it accurate, current and used properly?
Every fact comes only from the 571-row sourced Golden Record — the model is never allowed to state a fact it wasn't handed. When nothing matches well enough, or a customer's tone signals real distress, it hands off to a human with a reference code instead of guessing: "To get the most accurate and up-to-date information on scheduled water outages or service updates in your area, please check directly with NAWASA's team: Hotline: (473) 440-2155 WhatsApp: 405 5245 / 459 6064 / 405 9143 Reference Code: WES-RYV0QV A representative will be able to look up your specific location and give you the latest outage schedules and updates." Never invents a fact; asks a clarifying question instead of picking for the customer when multiple matches are weak; won't take payment (Quick Bill Lookup is read-only, currently against a sample account list); won't guess eligibility; admin/analytics endpoints are password-protected; data-sovereignty is disclosed to users (Gemini processes messages outside Grenada); transcribed speech is never auto-sent, the customer reviews it first
